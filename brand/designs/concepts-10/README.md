# Design concepts 10: hoodies (5 Nov 2026)

Six unisex pullover hoodies in the owner's tee format: acid-fade blank, a small mark at the bottom left of the front beside the pocket, and the back print kept inside the back panel. The only words are FINAL HEAVEN (and 07 on the dirt bike). GPT Image 2.6, 1k, medium, 0.5 credit each. See `brand/designs/DTF-RULES.md`.

Before any of these go further:
- **hoodie-04:** the poster is one big solid block of colour, which prints stiff. Open it up (halftone or cut-outs) or drop the frame.
- **hoodie-06:** check that the body shading is solid dots, not grey tone. The figure is invented and must stay that way.
- **hoodie-03:** the white bubble letters are a large solid area too; distress them lightly.
- **All:** 1k mockups only. Store photos and print files come after the owner picks.
