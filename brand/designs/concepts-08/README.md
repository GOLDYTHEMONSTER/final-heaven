Before any of these go further:
- **Copy I wrote, needing the owner's approval:** "SEE YOU AT THE GATES.", "CHOIR OF THE STILL HERE", "LAGOS, ABOVE."
- **pair-01 to pair-03:** these show only the backs. The owner's rule is the mark on the front and the print on the back, so store photos need a front view too.
- **The mark:** the model redraws it, and the tall spike comes out shorter on some pieces. Print and embroidery artwork must use `brand/logo/`, not these renders.
- **pair-03:** check the skyline and buses stay generic, with no real company's livery.
- **ladies-15 and coord-02:** satin with lace, and fine knit, need a supplier sample and a price before they're offered.
- **Tees and hoodies in the set:** adding a new pair to the Drop 01 picker means adding it to `lib/drop-01.ts` and to the set product's `colors` in the store first.
