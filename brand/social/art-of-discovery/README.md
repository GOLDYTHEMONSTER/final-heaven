# The Art of Discovery: content series

## 01 · Break-out (Story / Reel cover, 1080 × 1920)

`01-breakout-story-1080x1920.png`. A Final Heaven post where the model leaps out of the frame: head, arms and the console rise above it, the kicked shoe breaks its left edge, the planted shoe drops below it. One look covers three categories (Final Heaven 07 Hoodie, Moissanite Cuban Chain, Retro Handheld Console), with a "Shop the look" strip giving the next step.

- Model photo generated on Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5) from the real product photos; cut-out with Higgsfield background removal. Both kept here (`-source-photo`, `-cutout`) for re-use in motion.
- Post frame, text and prices typeset, not generated. No like or comment counts: invented numbers would misrepresent engagement.
- Handle shown as `finalheaven.shop`; swap in the real Instagram handle if it differs.

Suggested caption: You came looking. You found something better. Hoodie, chain, console: all at finalheaven.shop. Link in bio.

### Reel (5 s, 1080 × 1920, with sound)

`01-breakout-reel-5s-1080x1920.mp4`: the Story image brought to life on Higgsfield (Cinema Studio Video, start frame = the Story). The page holds still; the model bursts through the post (it shatters), lands grinning close to the lens, and the chain swings past the camera, leaving the empty post behind. `01-breakout-reel-frames.png` shows eight frames from it. Loops well; post with the Story image as the cover.

A 10-second multi-shot version (chain and console close-ups) was also tried; the page text warped and the console changed shape, so it isn't used.

## 02 · How I copped the whole fit (Reel, 20 s, 1080 × 1920)

`02-how-to-cop-scene2-20s.mp4` continues the story: the model dives into a phone and, in his own bragging voice, walks through buying Drop 01 on the real store: 01 tap Drop 01, 02 pick your hoodie, 03 cap + size, 04 add the full set (₦80,000), 05 check out (card, bank transfer or USSD via Flutterwave), then "Saved ₦33,000. You're welcome." (the saving the site shows for that set) and the end card. `02-how-to-cop-frames.png` shows stills.

- Every phone screen is a real screen recording of the store (phone viewport), not generated. Analytics were blocked while recording and no order was placed.
- Transitions on every step: flash, zoom punch, colour-split glitch and a pink/amber light sweep, cut to the beat.
- Music: Scene 1's own track. One bar of its beat (150 BPM) loops under Scene 2 and it ends on Scene 1's closing hit, so it continues seamlessly.

`episode-01-full-25s.mp4` is Scene 1 + Scene 2 joined on the beat: the break-out, then the walkthrough. Post this as the Reel.
