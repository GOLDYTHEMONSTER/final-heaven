# Tee concepts, round 2 (22 Oct 2026)

Six back-print concepts for T-shirts. They take the visual language of trending graphic tees (sliced statue photos, a photo triptych, city coordinates with varsity type, stacked outline words, spray graffiti, sleeve prints) and use only Final Heaven's own words and mark. Mockups only, 1k: chosen designs need store photos and flat print files made, as Drop 01 did.

| File | Garment | Back print |
|---|---|---|
| `tee-05-sculpted.png` | Black | Marble angel face sliced into four strips, typewriter lines between, quote below, mark in a box; "FINAL HEAVEN" down the left sleeve |
| `tee-06-see-you-in-heaven.png` | Black | Vertical triptych (angel, wing, reaching hands), "SEE YOU IN HEAVEN" running down the side, mark below |
| `tee-07-lagos-danfo-club.png` | White | Lagos coordinates, varsity "LAGOS" with "Final Heaven" script, danfo minibus line art, "DANFO CLUB"; "LAGOS" on both sleeves |
| `tee-08-ascend.png` | Sage green | Three cloud-and-sunray panels, italic "Ascend" with the mark, "LIVE LIKE IT'S FINAL" |
| `tee-09-heaven-smoke.png` | Brown | "HEAVEN" solid then three times in outline, smoke cherub flying through; smoke wing on the left sleeve |
| `tee-10-spray-mark.png` | White | The mark sprayed huge with drips, five stars under the collar, "Final Heaven" graffiti tag with a crown, label patch at the hem |

To approve before production:
- **tee-05**: the lines "less noise, / more meaning, / same soul." and the quote "Everything that was not needed was removed." were written by the model.
- **tee-08**: "HIGHER THOUGHTS / BIGGER TOMORROWS" and "GREATER PURPOSE / DEEPER ROOTS / BIGGER DREAMS / LASTING IMPACT" were written by the model.
- **tee-07**: the first render had a Volkswagen badge on the van; it was removed. The van is still shaped like the classic VW minibus the real danfos are, which is fine as a generic illustration, but keep it badge-free.
