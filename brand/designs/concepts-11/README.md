# Design concepts 11: crewneck sweatshirts (8 Nov 2026)

Three crewnecks in the style of the owner's references (script down the sleeves, a hem script with sparkles, tonal tribal flames), drawn on the real washed crewneck blanks from Suning Sufang Yazhi Clothing Co. (see `../WORKFLOW.md`). GPT Image 2.6, 1k, medium, 0.5 credit each. The only words are "Final Heaven", and the small mark sits at the bottom left of the front.

Before production:
- **Sleeve prints** are separate placements, one per sleeve. Ask the printer for the longest sleeve print they can press (about 45–50 cm).
- **crew-02:** the halftone edge must be solid dots at print size, not a soft fade.
- **crew-03:** large coverage. Keep it to one flat tonal ink so it stays soft, and split it into chest, left sleeve and right sleeve files.
- The crewneck's own listing and price aren't confirmed yet.
