# How we make Final Heaven designs

Read this with `DTF-RULES.md` before generating anything. The aim is to get it right in one pass, because every adjustment round costs credits.

## The format the owner chose

- Any theme, but no "Lagos" and no taglines or joke captions. The art leads, and the only words are FINAL HEAVEN (a number like 07 is fine).
- The three-star mark from `brand/logo/` always goes small, at the bottom left of the front, above the hem (beside the pocket on a hoodie).
- **The mark is embroidered, not printed** (owner, 8 Nov). Use raised satin-stitch thread with visible stitches, about 5–6 cm tall, in a thread colour that suits the blank. A printed mark next to a print looked flat and out of place. Prompt it as "embroidered in raised satin-stitch thread, visible stitch texture". In production this is a second process (digitised embroidery) on top of the DTF print, so get it costed.
- **One mark per side:** when the embroidered mark is on the front, the front print must not contain the three-star mark again: no hidden marks, no mark-shaped sparkles, no mark panels. A mark inside a back print is fine (owner, 8 Nov).
- **Price:** a piece with the embroidered mark costs ₦5,000 more than the same piece with a printed one (owner, 8 Nov). Keep the mark small (about 4 cm) and identical on every piece: same three-star shape, satin stitch, at the bottom left.
- Washed or acid-fade blanks.
- The print stays inside the body: about 30 cm wide on an adult L, roughly 10 cm below the collar or hood seam, clear of the seams, sleeves, pocket and ribbing.

## Realistic fit, first time

- **Use the real blank as the reference.** Upload the supplier's photo of the exact blank and colour we'll buy, and tell the model to keep its cut, proportions, drop shoulders, hood, ribbing, wash and colour. Never let the model invent the garment: invented blanks come out boxy and cartoon-sized.
- Give the print a real size ("about 30 cm wide on an adult size L"), not "70 percent of the body".
- Show the garment flat with natural folds, on #ECEAE6, filling about 85% of the frame.

## Spending credits

1. **Concepts:** 1k, quality medium (0.5 credit each), at most 3 at a time. Use a real blank as the reference from the start.
2. **Store photos:** 2k (1 credit each), only for designs the owner picked. Generate the back (the design). The plain front (blank and small mark) is the cheaper side: make it last, or reuse one front per colour across designs.
3. Don't regenerate for small fixes the owner hasn't asked for. List the issue in the README instead.
