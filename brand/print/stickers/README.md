## Printing

- Ask for vinyl, gloss or matte laminate, with a kiss-cut (or die-cut singles) along the magenta lines.
- Laminated vinyl lasts on phones and bottles. Plain paper stickers will peel and fade.
- Paper-colour stickers (`#F2F1EE`) look off-white. On clear vinyl, ask the printer for a white underprint.
- Check the QR code with a phone on the printer's proof before running the job.
