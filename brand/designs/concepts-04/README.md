# Design concepts 04: jackets and tracksuits (24 Oct 2026)

Mockups generated with GPT Image 2.5 on Higgsfield from the owner's reference board: a puffer gilet, a retro football track jacket, a varsity jacket, a monogram bomber and a classic track jacket. Every reference carried another brand's logos (The North Face, adidas, a football federation crest). None of those appear here; the three-star mark came from `brand/logo/mark-black.png` as a reference.

## The broken-star tape

The owner asked for sleeve stripes "like adidas", with Final Heaven's single star in a broken pattern. adidas's three stripes are a registered trademark, and adidas has also gone after two- and four-stripe lookalikes. So the tape is **one** stripe, broken into short dashes, with a single four-pointed star from the mark in the gaps. It reads as sport tape from a distance and as Final Heaven up close. Keep it to one tape per sleeve or leg; never add parallel stripes alongside it.
